HealthScale provides a centralized interface for registering patients and monitoring their health information. Each patient receives a unique Patient ID automatically.

Cloud Architecture

The HealthScale platform is designed to integrate with AWS services such as EC2 Auto Scaling, Application Load Balancer and CloudWatch for scalable healthcare monitoring.
